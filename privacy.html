Privacy Policy

Roadwork: Home Boxing Workout · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Roadwork app (the “App”) handles, why, and what choices you have. The App is published by Kartal Akyildiz, an individual developer based in Turkey (“I”, “me”). The short version: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your training data stays on your iPhone.

1. Summary

  • No account, login or cloud sync. I do not run a server that stores your data.
  • Your training progress, setup answers and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or HealthKit.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no cross-app tracking, and I never sell your data.
  • Subscription purchases go through Apple and are validated by RevenueCat, a payment infrastructure provider.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following in a local database on your iPhone. It is not sent to me or to anyone else.

  • Setup answers: training goal, boxing experience, stance (orthodox or southpaw), training equipment, preferred session length and training days, movement preferences, your chosen coach and fighter look.
  • Training progress: completed sessions and rounds, experience points (XP), rank, streaks and quest completion dates.
  • Settings and flags: for example whether music is on, which first-run tips you have seen, and whether the App has already asked for a rating.

During setup the App also asks for your height and weight. These are used on screen only to show a boxing weight class as part of the experience. They are not used for health purposes, they are not medical data, and they are not transmitted off your device.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier that I can link to you.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

Roadwork offers auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive your card or payment details, or your Apple ID.

To manage subscriptions, the App uses RevenueCat, a third-party service that processes purchase information on my behalf. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated for your installation, together with purchase and receipt information from Apple (for example, which product you bought and whether your subscription is active), and basic technical information such as device type, OS version and country, so it can confirm your access. RevenueCat uses this information to validate purchases and entitlements. Its handling of data is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. I only share data with RevenueCat for this purpose, and I expect it to protect that data to the standard described in this policy.

“Restore Purchases” asks Apple and RevenueCat to re-confirm your subscription. It restores access to the App, not your training progress.

4. Audio and device features

Coach voices, sound effects and music are bundled inside the App. The App does not stream audio and does not record sound. It can keep your screen awake during a session and keep audio playing when the screen is locked. The App may ask Apple’s standard system dialog to rate the App; ratings are handled by Apple and I do not receive information about you from that dialog.

5. What I do not do

  • I do not use advertising or show third-party ads.
  • I do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • I do not sell or rent personal data and I do not share it for advertising.
  • I do not access HealthKit or any health records.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Deleting the App from your iPhone permanently removes your training progress, setup answers and settings; I cannot recover them, because I do not have a copy. Reinstalling the App starts you from a fresh state.

Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own policies and legal obligations. If you want RevenueCat’s record associated with your anonymous app user ID deleted, or want to ask any question about your data, email me at roadwork.support@tickerand.ink and I will act on the request and forward it to the relevant provider where needed. Because I cannot identify you from your device alone, I may need to ask you for additional information (for example an Apple order receipt) to locate the record.

Because no account exists, there is nothing to sign out of or delete on my side beyond the items above. You can revoke any consent by deleting the App; you can stop future collection of purchase and analytics information the same way.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to me, contact me and I will help resolve it. The physical exercises in the App are intended for adults and for teenagers with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian.

8. International users

I am based in Turkey. Third-party providers mentioned above (Apple, RevenueCat) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. To exercise them, contact me at the address below.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the App’s private storage on your device and protected by the security features of iOS. Communication with RevenueCat uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but I keep the amount of data handled to a minimum.

10. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date above shows the current version. If a change is significant I will say so in the App or in the App Store release notes.
