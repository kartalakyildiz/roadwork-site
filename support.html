Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Roadwork and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen or in Settings. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that bought the subscription (Settings > your name). Restoring brings back your subscription access. It does not restore training progress; see below.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside Roadwork. On your iPhone: Settings > tap your name > Subscriptions > Roadwork > Cancel Subscription (or Cancel Free Trial). To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How does the free trial work?

If the yearly plan shows a free trial in the app, you will see how long it lasts and what is charged afterwards before you start. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends if you do not want to be charged. Any unused part of the trial is forfeited if you buy a subscription during it.

I was charged and want a refund.

Apple processes all payments and refunds, and I cannot issue refunds directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something looks wrong with your subscription status in the app, write to me and I will help.

Where is my training progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Roadwork has no account and no cloud sync. Your progress, XP, rank and settings are saved only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted, and it does not move to another phone automatically. Restore Purchases restores access, not progress. Keep the app installed to keep your progress.

I can’t hear the coach, or the audio is wrong.
  • Turn the volume up with the side buttons.
  • Silent mode: Roadwork is designed to play the coach voice even when the ringer switch is on silent. If you still hear nothing, flip the switch to ring, raise the volume and try again.
  • Bluetooth: if your phone is connected to a speaker, car or earbuds, audio plays there. Disconnect or reconnect the device, or pick the output in Control Center.
  • Phone calls, other audio apps and navigation prompts can interrupt playback. Close other audio apps and start the round again.
  • You can turn the background music off in Settings; the coach voice stays on.
  • If the app still has no sound, force-quit it and reopen. If that fails, email me with your iPhone model and iOS version.
Does the app work offline?

Coach voices and music are bundled inside the app, so training audio does not need an internet connection. An internet connection is needed to buy, restore or verify a subscription.

What data does Roadwork collect?

No account is needed and your training data stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details, including how to ask for deletion of purchase records.

Is Roadwork safe for me?

Roadwork is fitness content, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting, stop if you feel pain or dizziness, and make sure you have a clear, safe space to train. See the Terms of Use.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL@example.com. Bug reports with steps to reproduce are the most helpful.
